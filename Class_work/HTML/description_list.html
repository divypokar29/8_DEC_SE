Narendra Modi
Narendra Damodardas Modi is an Indian politician who has served as the prime minister of India since 2014. Modi was the chief minister of Gujarat from 2001 to 2014 and is the member of parliament for Varanasi.
Rahul Gandhi
Rahul Gandhi is an Indian politician and a member of the Indian National Congress. He has served as the president of the Indian National Congress from 2017 to 2019.
